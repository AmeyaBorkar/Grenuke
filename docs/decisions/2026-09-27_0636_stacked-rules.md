- **Date (IST):** 2026-09-27 06:36
- **Author:** ameya

- **Affects:**

## Context

- **The threshold was already optimal.** Tuning it on the US/India holdout overfits: repeated 2-fold CV gives −18.8e-6 out of sample. v7nst's 0.675 is the full-holdout optimum.
- **The plain expected-F0.5 rule looked like a tie.** RESEARCH_v6.md §6.15 measured it at +0.00003 [−0.00002, +0.00007] against the threshold on v7mst.
- **France's remaining loss is substitutions** (§6.14, §6.15), but two French populations had never been checked:
  - exact copies that the model misses, mostly French abbreviations such as Cie/Compagnie;
  - cross-commune pairs from the acronym join's loose address test ("small leak, not fixed").
- On 27 Sep, three analysis agents (hunt, polish, decide) searched these on the US/India holdout and on label-free French evidence. They reported paired bootstraps (`ber.eval.gates`, 1,000 resamples) with the fold-parity halves A/B.

## Options considered

US/India holdout, v7s (threshold 0.70, F0.5 0.991229); Δ ×1e-6:

| rules on the labelled countries | full [95% CI] | P(better) | A | B | US | India |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| threshold + acr + cap + nsa (hunt) | +9.4 [+1.7, +17.5] | 0.990 | +10.0 | +8.6 | +7.8 | +11.9 |
| + rank0 (empty-S1 rescue, pc 0.6–0.70) | +33.1 [+9.4, +56.3] | 0.996 | +18.1 | +55.6 | +24.5 | +46.0 |
| expected-F0.5 per S1 (shift +0.2, phantom 0.01) | +33.2 [−7.5, +75.0] | 0.945 | −7.8 | +94.7 | +58.2 | −4.3 |
| + acr + cap | +35.9 [−4.8, +77.6] | 0.958 | −4.8 | +97.1 | +60.2 | −0.5 |
| **+ crowd shift −0.3 (records with ≥ 4 S1 at p1 ≥ 0.02)** | **+48.1 [+7.1, +91.2]** | **0.987** | +7.7 | +108.7 | +70.8 | +13.9 |

- **Selection:**
  - The DP shift and phantom were selected on v7nst's half A and confirmed on its half B (+45.5).
  - The crowd shift was selected on v7s's half A from {0.15, 0.3, 0.45} and confirmed on its half B.
  - Repeated 2-fold CV of the DP with the phantom (v7nst, 42 splits): +23.7 out of sample, positive in 86% of splits. The DP part is positive on all 5 models tested.
- **France** (no labels; the evidence is population truth rates measured on US/India, and French rates measured label-free):
  - `copy`: add an unpredicted pair with equal names (up to legal forms, stop words, word order, Cie/Ets/St/Ste), the exact same address and the same numbers, where the S1 is the record's best candidate and the record is free. This population is 99.992% true (US) and 99.998% true (India). v7s already predicts 99.90% of the French one, and the rule adds the rest: +332 on v7s.
  - `city`: drop a pair whose S1 and record name different communes. French true copies change commune in 3 of 546,465 pairs, so a drop is about 95% false; break-even is 35%. −89 on v7s.
  - The hunt's `acr` for France is kept only when the record address has no digit. The 5 French adds with another house number or street are nudged look-alikes. The hunt's French `cap` drops are left out: they include perfect copies at pc 0.997–1.000.
  - `fhs.py` against v7s: the hunt's own French changes score −0.02; the narrowed hunt plus polish score +1.09.

## Decision

- **Apply the whole stack to every final candidate:**
  - US/India: expected-F0.5 with the crowd shift, plus `acr` and `cap`;
  - France: the narrowed hunt plus `copy` and `city`.
  - This is the `-dpc` package, built by `stack/stack.sh <model>`. The `-h2pc` package (threshold + acr + cap + nsa) is the conservative fallback.

## Consequences (what changes, what we give up, how we'll know it was right)

- **Expected leaderboard gain on v7sq, over v7nst's 0.990179:**
  - US/India part +0.000048 (the model) and about +0.00004 (the rules);
  - France rules about +0.00003;
  - the model's French changes are unknown in sign.
  - Expected total: about 0.9903.
- **Right if** the leaderboard score is above v7nst's by more than the ±0.00004 noise between near-identical candidates.
- **Wrong if** it falls below about 0.9902. That would mean the French side regressed (the model's changes or the rules), and the fallback is `v7nst-dpc`.
- **What we give up:**
  - the hunt's `rank0`, which the DP subsumes;
  - `nsa` as a hard band, replaced by the crowd shift in the calibration;
  - any French use of `cap` and `nsa`, whose evidence depends on calibration measured only on US/India.
- **Hand-written lexicons only:** Cie/Compagnie, Ets/Etablissements, St/Saint, Ste/Sainte, and US state names as regions. No models, no external data.
