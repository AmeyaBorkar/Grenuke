## Context

The v6all research round (`RESEARCH_v6.md` §2.7) hand-reviewed French candidate lists and found about 1,291 op-B look-alikes that the France rules still predict (5.0 per 1000 French S1). The rules test "at the S1's own address" with the (house number, first street word) key. French addresses break that key:
- suffixed numbers: "8BIS", "129 D", "51 T";
- typo'd street types: "AVEUNE", "ASLEE", "Pace";
- abbreviations: "Q." for Quai;
- two-letter typos in short street names: "Arts" / "Arst".

Stage 3 (joint re-scoring of contested records plus record-mass calibration of empty-address records) was +0.00005 on v5all and not yet run on v6all.

## Options considered

1. **Rules v3:** a robust same-address test added to the key test.
   - The first house number is read without its suffix.
   - The street is the set of words after it, with street types (exact or up to a typo), articles and suffixes skipped.
   - A match needs equal numbers and one shared street word up to a typo.

   US/India holdout (`post_ops.py --measure --robust-addr --matches ameya-model-v6all-c2 --feats ameya-fx5`), pairs matched only by the new test:

   | population | India: pairs, true | US: pairs, true | action in France |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | B (a real word swapped in the slot) | 3,178, **0.0%** | 406, **1.2%** | drop |
   | A (word dropped, list word appended) | 1,305, 87.4% | 4,037, 99.1% | add |
   | APP (list word appended) | 468, 97.0% | 851, 89.4% | add |
   | ACR (initials) | 48, 95.8% | 27, 100% | add |

   Adding pays above about 72% precision (a false positive costs about 0.18 F0.5, a miss about 0.07).
2. **Stage 3 on v6all** (`stage3.py --scores ameya-s2-v6all --tag ameya-s3-v6all --p-cand 0.02 --top-r 2`, then `decide.py`):
   - holdout 0.990842 vs 0.990788;
   - **Δ +0.000055 [+0.000025, +0.000085]**, p_better 1.0 (US +0.000052, India +0.000059);
   - the threshold moves to 0.675 (G6 keeps the threshold: DP +0.00004 [−0.00001, +0.00008]).
3. Per-record renormalisation (pc / max(1, Σ pc over the record's S1)): holdout +0.000015. Not taken, too small to matter.

## Consequences (what changes, what we give up, how we'll know it was right)

- **Expected change against `v6all-ops-c2`:**
  - about +0.00005 from US/India (stage 3);
  - about +0.001 France F0.5 from rules v3 (+0.00015 on the leaderboard).
- **Right if** LB(v6all-s3-ops3-c2) ≥ LB(v6all-ops-c2).
- **Wrong if** it drops. Then France's newly matched pairs behave unlike the holdout: go back to `v6all-ops-c2`.
- **The fr090 probe prices France's uncertain band.** France predicts 186 pairs per 1000 S1 at pc 0.7–0.99, against 52 in US/India.
  - If it scores above the candidate, the band's precision is below about 0.72 and France needs a stricter threshold.
  - If below, the band is calibrated and the remaining France loss is in confident predictions.
