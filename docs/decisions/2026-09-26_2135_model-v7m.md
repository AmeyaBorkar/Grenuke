## Context

- v7ce3 (e5-small, e5-base and e5-large as three separate stage-2 features) passed its gate at +0.0003.
- Two more cross-encoders were trained on the same band and OOF groups (`RESEARCH_v6.md` §6.5):
  - e5-large, 2 epochs: band AUC 0.9441 on the holdout;
  - bge-reranker-v2-m3 (Apache-2.0, 568M): 0.9418.
- On US/India test pairs the cross-encoders agree almost perfectly (correlation 0.98, sign disagreement 3%). On France they diverge: correlation 0.91, sign disagreement 12% (§6.6).
- A stage 2 that takes each logit separately learns its splits where the models agree, then extrapolates on their disagreements in France.
