# Experiments: bakshi

Every measured attempt by Bakshi or his agents, kept or not (`knowledge/STANDARD.md` §2.2). Local IDs are `B-X-NN`, in time order. "Codex" and "Claude" name which of his agents ran it.

**Summary.** 50 attempts. On 25–26 Sep: a prototype, normalisation and string features (merged, unused) and a France rule audit that found nothing to ship. On 27 Sep morning: the package auditor and builder, the cross-encoder weighting test and six screens that all came back negative. From 13:30: the rented-GPU rebuild, Qwen2.5-7B, the 7B re-check and Composite B (public LB 0.990879), then a French drop ladder that tied (B7 0.990875).

Scope words: **local holdout** = the fixed 25% of labelled US/India S1 (549,699 S1); **dev** = the team dev kit (a sample of train); **test** = label-free counts on the test set; **public LB** = the leaderboard.
