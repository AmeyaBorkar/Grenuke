- **Who.** "Agent for Ameya" is the Claude Code agent working in his sessions: the main session (`19e315ba`), a parallel research session (`a2a1b62a`) and a side session (`c0c64ad6`). A "sub-agent" is an agent that it launched.
- **Decisions** are named by title, with who decided in brackets. The curator maps the titles to IDs.
- **Numbers** are measured [M] unless tagged [E] estimated or [R] reported. "Holdout" is the local holdout: a fixed 25% of labelled US/India S1 (549,699 S1), with no France. "LB" is the public leaderboard.
- **Terms.** S1 is the reference list of businesses; S2 and S3 are two vendors' listings, and the task is to list, for each S1, the S2/S3 records of the same business. F0.5 is the precision-weighted score. Blocking is the cheap first step that produces the candidate pairs. Stages 0, 1, 2 and 3 are the model cascade (stage 3 re-scores contested records). The DP picks, per S1, the set with the best expected F0.5. A cross-encoder (CE) is a text model that reads two records together. Self-training means training on the model's own confident decisions for France, which has no labels. LOCO is leave-one-country-out. Op-A and op-B are two edits of the French data (list-word appends, mostly true; in-place descriptor swaps, mostly decoys). A decoy looks like a match but is a different business. More terms are in [`timeline.md`](../../timeline.md).
