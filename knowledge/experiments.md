# Experiments ledger

This ledger lists every measured attempt of team Grenuke from 25 Sep to 29 Sep 2026, kept or not: **329 experiments**, EXP-001 to EXP-329, merged from twelve overlapping source extracts. 177 were kept, 126 dropped, 17 inconclusive and 9 superseded.
France, the country that never appears in train, drew the most work (69). The sources hold no measured attempts from 3 Oct on (phase P6).
Format and areas follow [STANDARD §2.2 and §4](STANDARD.md). Times are IST.

### The 15 that mattered most

1. EXP-019: Features v1, stage 0 and out-of-fold stage 1 took holdout F0.5 from 0.9683 to 0.9781 (+0.0097), the biggest single holdout step.
2. EXP-029: Look-alike word odds and cluster support: +0.00431 over model v1, India +0.0100. It showed that business look-alikes, not recall, were the main enemy.
3. EXP-038: Legal-form relation features separated look-alikes from copies: holdout +0.00271 and about 20% lower stage-2 log-loss.
4. EXP-056: The leaderboard read of model v3 showed a holdout-to-LB gap of −0.0092. It pointed at France and redirected the project.
5. EXP-062: Leave-one-country-out (US → India) simulated France: unseen look-alike words cost about −0.10. It explained the gap and led to the label-free proxy odds.
6. EXP-059: An e5-small cross-encoder as a stage-2 feature: holdout +0.00140 and singletons 0.9913 → 0.9974. It started the cross-encoder line.
7. EXP-078: Generator ops A and B: France accepted descriptor swaps that are false in US/India. It was the root cause behind the France rules.
8. EXP-105: The v6all rebuild (blocking v3 repairs, signed number features, candidate cut, rules v2): holdout +0.00063. It became the base of every later model.
9. EXP-159: Averaged large cross-encoders: LB +0.001112, the biggest leaderboard jump after v3, with France 0.973 → 0.978 [E].
10. EXP-161: Stage-2 self-training on France: LB +0.000458, from 31 changed predictions per 1,000 French S1.
11. EXP-222: French self-trained cross-encoders (Qwen2.5-1.5B, e5-large) plus the stack: LB +0.000366 over v7nst (model +0.000281, stack +0.000085).
12. EXP-268: A Qwen2.5-7B LoRA cross-encoder, self-trained on French labels: band AUC 0.9436; inside the stage-2 mix India +66e-6 (P 0.998).
13. EXP-250: The negative that framed the 7B: zero-shot Qwen2.5-7B scored AUC 0.537 against 0.898 for pc, while the self-trained 7B scored 0.9436.
14. EXP-288: Dropping confident predictions that the 7B rejects (logit −6): holdout-sample +0.000033, confirmed on the full holdout (+0.000037).
15. EXP-309: Composite B (g1w US/India + mixmdp France − 7B rejects): LB 0.990879, the best upload.

### How to read the ledger

- **Evaluations.** "holdout" is the local holdout: the fixed 25% of labelled US/India S1 (549,699 S1; no France). "dev" is dev fold 0 (27,651 S1). "LB" is the public leaderboard. "label-free" means a count on the test set without labels: it shows what the model does, not how well. The private leaderboard is never quoted.
- **Levels.** [M] measured on labels or scored by the leaderboard; [E] estimated or derived without labels; [R] reported and not re-checked; [U] uncertain (also listed in section 3). "[M, label-free]" is a direct count without labels.
- **Verdicts.** kept: the change was adopted, or the finding stands and was used. dropped: the idea was tested and rejected, or the suspicion was refuted. inconclusive: no usable result or no decision. superseded: replaced by a later version of the same idea (named).
- **Who.** "agent for Ameya" is an AI agent working under Ameya's direction; a sub-agent carries its short id. Ameya, Bakshi and Sachi are named where the source names them.
- **Terms.** S1 is a source-1 business record; S2 and S3 are the other two vendors' records. F0.5 is the macro F0.5 per S1 (precision counts double). Δ is a paired-bootstrap difference over S1 with a 95% interval in brackets (1,000 resamples). e-6 means a millionth of F0.5. OOF is out-of-fold. p1 and pc are the stage-1 probability and the calibrated stage-2 probability. Stage 3 is joint re-scoring. DP is the expected-F0.5 decision per S1. c2 and s3 are the holdout score after stage 2 plus the decision, and after stage 3 plus the decision. CE is a cross-encoder. "Band" is the uncertain probability band that the cross-encoders re-read. e5l, e5ls, bge, qst and q7st are the large e5, French self-trained e5, bge re-ranker, 1.5B Qwen and 7B Qwen cross-encoders. cal, fhs and "units" are label-free estimators of a French change's worth on the LB (cal is the best: scale 0.96, mean error 42e-6). Op A is a French copy edit and op B a look-alike swap. A look-alike is a business record that copies an S1 with a small change (a nudged house number, an added word) but is not the same business. "~" in the time column means the source gives only a date or an approximate time; the row is placed among its neighbours.
